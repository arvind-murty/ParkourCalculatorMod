# Contact combination implementation

2026-10-29. `contactEvents` is the common route contract for finite X/Y/Z contacts. Each event has `leg`, input `offset`, signed `direction`, actual `box`, expanded `plane` and optional `role`: contact, reversal, climb, ceiling, landing or step. A role never changes Minecraft collision or ladder-carry rules. Only reversal adds the next-input outward row. Events may occur on the final landing-producing input. Compatible duplicate axis contacts merge; conflicting planes/directions reject the fixed skeleton.

`avoidBefore` and `avoidAfter` carry relative positional rows; `avoidOffsets` selects normal-side intervals. Existing signed blockage chains remain independently composable with these contacts and keep per-physical-block H2/V2/combined3 caps. Exact absence of unselected hits is checked by the shared scene. Finite Y contacts use old X/Z; X uses resolved Y/old Z; Z uses resolved Y/new X. Explicit steps require the actual shared step resolver, matching finite top and resulting overlap, rather than pretending an upward step is a normal downward clip.

`contact_combinations(opportunities, max_events=3, limit=1000, ladder_windows=...)` enumerates all compatible axis subsets under an explicit work cap. Normal coupled choices span one input or two consecutive inputs. A declared ladder window admits the full sequence. Independent normal events separated by free inputs remain separate choices, carried in the complete route solve. Size families are interleaved before caps. No reversal-direction or rarity-based route pruning is added.

`contact_profile` replays the complete chosen horizontal/Y contact and ladder/Sneak sequence through the existing Y recurrence and derives the actual landing duration. `generate_contact_profiles` combines opportunities and regenerates profiles. Same-input ceiling/climb can refresh upward carry after the ceiling cancellation; repeated ceiling/climb and the final omitted climb hit are covered. Disconnected occupied intervals can be represented without a fictitious landing, jump or carry reset.

Collision-box route search now includes bounded generated contact profiles, controlled by `maxContactCombinations` (64 inspected subsets per height pair by default, zero disables this additional profile family) and `maxCombinedEvents` (3 by default). Existing single-wall/ceiling families remain available. These are work limits, not exclusions of particular contact classes; bounded enumeration can miss useful combinations. Automatic ladder/multiple-region composition still follows the broader work in progress in LADDER_COMPOSITION_PLAN.md. Do not confuse primitive support with completed automatic discovery of every mixed ladder strategy.

Verification: 12 contact-combination tests plus 39 existing generator tests passed. Geometry checks cover all single-input horizontal axis subsets with no Y hit, a ceiling hit or a landing, mirrored through both normal signs. All 63 nonempty subsets of X/Y/Z events over two inputs are enumerated. Full ladder-window combinations, free-gap separation, conflicting events, relative retiming, final-input contacts, repeated ceiling/climb carry and actual step acceptance have coverage. Java-enabled non-IPOPT regression discovery passed 187 tests with 26 integration cases skipped; this is not the complete numerical gate.

The geometry-only `xyz-contact-corner.json` cold search accepted a three-input route in about 8.6 seconds with ceiling plus Z clipping on input 0 and X clipping on input 1. The saved result in experiments/contact-combinations/corner-result.json retains full shared-scene acceptance. This is a witness for the coupled two-input case, not a global-optimality statement.
