## Problem and evidence

A preliminary solve can deliberately omit a future interaction, but current post-solve corridor and readiness checks still impose the full route. More generally, a complete candidate can reach IPOPT before its relevant obstacle choices are selected. The orchestrator then discovers missing rows after solving and can process unrelated inherited repairs first.

The exact Real11 reproduction runs24 distinct fixed problems, varying an unnecessary late B2 exit gate. None includes B6 avoidance. All24 returned trajectories first fail the corridor at input59 and independently collide with B6. New late repairs are queued but not dispatched. This is not evidence that24 genuine B6 alternatives were needed. Separately, X728.9 is an artificial bound copied from a helper waypoint. See [the exact audit](experiments/linkcraft11-opening/audit/CORRIDOR_FAILURES.md).

## Implementation sequence

1. **Make the solve's claimed requirements explicit.** Attach preliminary versus complete purpose, modeled physical interactions, intended route requirements and their time spans to a fixed candidate. A preliminary solve may omit B6; its projection, landmark and checkpoint/readiness checks must not silently require B6. Full geometry replay of deliberately omitted future interactions may be diagnostic but cannot invalidate the preliminary model result. Complete accepted witnesses still require the entire declared scene. Do not use the existing partial=True flag as this contract: it only permits a prefix of the landmark signature and still checks the whole horizontal trace.
2. **Remove artificial route bounds.** Reference points choose topology/direction, not maximum excursions. Stop clipping feasible regions to a waypoint envelope. Include neighboring free cells consistent with the route: merely removing the envelope still leaves Real11's selected-cell minimum X728.7. Use real obstacle/coverage/route boundaries and record their provenance. Explain B6 with a left region X<=729.7, central strip729.7..731.3 with Z<=-1095.3 and right region X>=731.3, while respecting other solids and vertical phase. Current declared scene permits player centers to X726.3;720 would require wider scene coverage. Preserve union semantics rather than intersecting every region's inequalities.
3. **Use one shared interaction-reachability helper before selecting alternatives.** Precompute conservative envelopes for the fixed movement/constraint context and query existing block-interaction constraint bundles at the correct ticks/phases. Reuse this helper for target opportunities, unwanted contacts and specific avoid-side branches. A ruled-out required interaction rejects its candidate; a ruled-out unwanted contact needs no avoidance; an unreachable avoid-side choice rejects only that choice. Not ruled out never means proved feasible. Do not implement separate target-block and obstacle-avoid screens. Use the fixed scalar Y/contact history and the actual Y->X->Z order. Above a block's top, its horizontal avoidance is unnecessary; below it, conservatively consider player-body overlap, swept movement and possible floor/ceiling contacts. Reuse existing finite-contact/support builders at the correct pre-horizontal phase. Then use conservative horizontal reachable envelopes, from the original state and fixed movement model, to exclude distant solids. A backward envelope from selected contacts may tighten this. Only omit a constraint when the entire reachable envelope is disjoint; distance from one optimized or reference trajectory is insufficient. If a conservative test is unavailable, retain the possibility. Do not add a post-horizontal support pin that would forbid valid edge jumps. Concrete algorithm and checked B2 examples are in [OBSTACLE_RELEVANCE.md](OBSTACLE_RELEVANCE.md); the immediate0 schedule excludes every early B2 interaction, but the coarse bound retains inputs15..19 for slow5.
4. **Select genuinely discrete constraints before each ordinary candidate call.** Reuse existing passage/normal/stepping/ceiling/contact builders to enumerate relevant side and transition-tick choices within the spatial route. For Real11, derive ordinary support constraints, eliminate the vertically irrelevant late B2 exit, then select the relevant B6 passage timing for a complete candidate. A preliminary solve can instead explicitly omit the future B6 requirement under step1. Each complete input+interaction alternative becomes a fixed continuous problem; numerical seed retries are recorded separately. Keep all earlier facings free and re-solve from the original full state.
5. **Keep inherited work compatible with the current problem.** Key cached/queued repairs by the physical program, route, modeled scope and chosen interaction requirements. Remove obsolete restrictions and preserve genuinely different pending alternatives. Do not let a stale B2 exit timing substitute for the new B6 decision. Save the outgoing terminal repair frontier and outer progress rather than regenerating old finals. These persistence/scheduling issues are related prerequisites, not a reason to inflate candidate counts.
6. **Keep verification separate from constraint discovery.** For a complete candidate, corridor checks verify the selected route and physical replay verifies actual movement. If supplied constraints are satisfied but the selected requirements fail, log a missing-constraint/model discrepancy. A route-only rejection with passing scene replay is evidence to review the route representation or preserve another spatial route. Do not silently promote preliminary feasibility to final physical feasibility. Continue retaining close alternatives under the user's existing margins and schedule/velocity policy; this fix does not prove their dominance assumptions.

## Logging milestone, implemented

Rejected corridor results now retain the corridor/projection/signature and hash, exact failing input and movement phase or expected/actual landmark token, supplied plan/walls, trajectory and vertical model, selected passage/hit branches, extra repair rows, numerical problem hashes and offered repair alternatives. Each record states whether physical replay was skipped. No additional numerical or physical replay calls are introduced by logging.

The actual current scope is recorded honestly as projection=whole_trajectory, partialSignature matching the caller, and modeledInteractionScope=unspecified. The explicit preliminary/complete scope in step1 is not implemented yet. Per-reason summary counts include callback audits; saved per-branch records describe returned candidates. They are not interchangeable with worker request counts. See the [runbook](CONSTRAINT_GENERATION_RUNBOOK.md#corridor-diagnostics-and-future-test-review) for inspection and replay.

## Acceptance checks for the behavioral fix

- A preliminary B6-omitted solve is not rejected by a B6 projection, signature or readiness requirement; already-modeled earlier requirements remain enforced.
- The same trajectory in a complete B6-modeled candidate is checked against that requirement and the full physical scene.
- A feasible route taking more leftward space than a representative waypoint remains available within declared coverage, including across neighboring compatible cells.
- Fixed-Y exclusion preserves valid landings, ceilings, steps and normal edge jumps. A distant-solid exclusion uses a conservative reachable envelope and preserves all candidates in its small exhaustive control.
- Real11 no longer dispatches the old24 late-B2 variants with missing B6 constraints. Record each fixed call's scope/rows and independently replay every accepted witness. Preserve the initial0/8 families and the existing objective/time margins. Report remaining unknowns without claiming a global optimum.
- Compare corridor failures with independently replayed physical outcomes on future maps, including a valid physical route rejected only by the corridor. Use that evidence to decide whether to keep, weaken or remove corridor checks; do not assume today's failures establish their general value.

No mod launch or Gradle gate is required for this standalone work. Use focused standalone checks and the existing shared Java scene helper. The new diagnostics and focused tests are complete; this document records the remaining work so it can be resumed independently.
