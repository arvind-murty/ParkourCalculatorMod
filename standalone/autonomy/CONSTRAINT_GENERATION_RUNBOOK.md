# Reusing the mod's block constraints in route discovery

2026-11-02. Reusable procedure established by the Linkcraft Real11 opening inventory and corner check. Use the existing movement, support, contact and collision builders. No second movement/constraint implementation is needed.

1. **Record physical geometry and coordinate conventions.** Convert each confirmed block/cell/column selection into physical AABBs[minX,minY,minZ,maxX,maxY,maxZ]. Singleton X/Z block coordinates span one unit. An inclusive cell range[a,b] spans physical[a,b+1]. A supplied physical Y range remains that range. Record uncertain shorthand before solving; confirmed user geometry supersedes shapes guessed from authored position rows. Start from the full original saved X/Y/Z position and velocity. Current research uses normal momentum with zero thresholds.
2. **Keep raw world boxes distinct from player-center regions.** The shared player half-width is0.30000001192092896. SupportOverlap supplies precise legal landing endpoints. Collision-space expansion and scene movement use the existing geometry helpers. A top Y is a support plane; it does not itself define a whole collision volume or a swept transition. Use only the declared geometry and state coverage/empty-space assumptions explicitly.
3. **Use the real neighbor-aware mod derivation for selected landing rectangles.** ConstraintKeyController.onKey obtains the support/collision boxes, reference point and facing, then calls ConstraintDeriver.deriveFootprint with nearby obstacles and the legacy/modern collision mode. Its SupportOverlap calculation includes player overhang; its obstacle/corner clipping removes body intersections. The standalone convenience footprints action currently passes obstacles=null, so it provides only unblocked support overlap. For an obstacle-aware report, invoke the existing Java helper with the actual neighbor list, as DeriveSelectedFootprints.java demonstrates. Do not expand already-authored player-center bounds a second time.
4. **Preserve unions at corners.** ConstraintDeriver returns one rectangle selected by reference/facing, not an exhaustive nonconvex region. On Real11 B1, the complete top area requires X>=742.3 OR Z<=-1104.3 within its expanded support rectangle. Keep both pieces as separate support regions/cells, retaining the same physical-solid owner, or keep the existing full cell decomposition. A GUI-selected rectangle cannot become the universal support domain. Splitting regions must not invent a physical wall along their shared seam. Existing cell_routes.geometry already preserves this corner and passes the generic union regression.
5. **Build the spatial route independently of event timing.** Use the actual AABBs and existing cell graph to define the chosen corridor. A pilot may choose an explicit support order as one route, but that is a route restriction to report, not a discovery guarantee over other routes. Guide points supply topology/direction, not fixed positions at recorded ticks. Do not copy TAS facings, prescribed airtimes or tactical scalar rows as cold-solve decisions. Reusing the saved initial state and a user-specified comparison tick is allowed.
6. **Generate each fixed discrete candidate with the existing catalogue.** Reuse normal/run/jump, stepping, ceiling, hit/avoid and blockage builders. Compose/retime their existing rows and finite contacts; select the branch before its IPOPT call. Nearby coupled choices may be generated jointly. Every later solve starts at the original state and leaves earlier facings free. In the present research scope exclude ladders, water, intentional wall-hit routes and inertia-threshold search.
7. **Validate required events and absence of unexpected events.** Use the shared coefficient compiler, scalar validator, finite scene/contact checker and corridor/readiness checks. Endpoint support overlap alone does not prove swept clearance. Existing contact/corridor repair may expose additional choices; keep unknowns distinct from impossibility and record unresolved branches. Do not redesign IPOPT to make discrete choices.
8. **Declare the objective precisely.** For fixed spatial goals compare accepted achievement times. For fixed absolute tick h compare the same horizontal objective at h. If the user removes a final landing, set terminalSupportRequired=false and compile an open prefix ending at h; do not leave a hidden final X/Z/Y/support constraint, do not stop at an earlier goal contact, and allow overshoot. Keep the physical final block as an obstacle/support opportunity. Other required route interactions remain enforced. Unmodeled mandatory floor contact cannot disappear merely because the final landing is optional.
9. **Retain and audit meaningful alternatives.** Use the separate time/progress results and conservative margins; preserve tradeoffs and saved work. Count caps are heuristics, not proofs. Do not increase them indiscriminately. Replay every accepted witness through the shared physics/scene helpers and compare against targeted additional-candidate or fresh-root joint references when evidence warrants it. Report supplied geometry/routes, unknowns and missing coverage explicitly.
