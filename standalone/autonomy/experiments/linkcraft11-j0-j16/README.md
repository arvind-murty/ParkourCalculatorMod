## Input/model construction

- Explicit initial position and zero horizontal stored velocity from the capture.
- 17 jump transitions, one run-off without jumping, one grounded step; supplied durations/contact phases.
- Force 45 effective key policy, with W-only sprint-jump inputs, and free facings everywhere.
- Normal 1.8.9 float friction and the standalone automatic per-axis .005 inertia rule.
- Landing/support rectangles, plus block-entry conditions for the short reduced hops and the stepped landing. These are generated by the planner from the declared support regions/contact phases; no F rows or copied scalar rule table.
- Physical block footprints use the existing Java ConstraintDeriver and SupportOverlap helpers.

The source save has no picked geometry. Landing rectangles are the supplied center regions. Two missing regions, the start block and first landing, are declared as normal grid-aligned full blocks based on their recorded positions. The platform before jump 10 is declared as the coplanar strip X=[733,734], Z=[-1090,-1088], consistent with its recorded grounded run-up. These fixture geometry assumptions are recorded in `summary.json`; they are not inferred from IPOPT output or presented as a world scan.

The full test also preserves conditions absent from the earlier short experiment: early entry onto the next block for reduced hops, the missed floor crossing before the stepped landing after jump 13, the larger standing platform before jump 10, and the explicit step before jump 15. This prevents merely deleting the late-jump conditions to get a cheaper solve.

## Reproduce

After preparing the Java compiler and owned IPOPT environment as in the parent README:

```sh
.venv-ipopt/bin/python -m standalone.autonomy.solve \
  --input standalone/autonomy/fixtures/linkcraft11-j0-j16.json \
  --output standalone/autonomy/build/linkcraft11-j0-j16-result.json \
  --seconds 180 --candidate-seconds 3 --retries 1
```

Timings and first-found allocation may vary. Regenerate the route fixture with `python3 -m standalone.autonomy.linkcraft --input /path/to/real/11.json --output /path/to/route.json --start 0 --end 130 --expanded`. The source capture SHA-256 is recorded in the fixture and summary.
